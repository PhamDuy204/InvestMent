# Research Questions

## Highest priority

1. Does H12 preserve **cross-sectional ordering** even when its absolute directional sign fails?
2. Which existing causal variables have a stable interaction with H12 sign across chronological development folds?
3. Is fold-1 failure caused by a change in market beta/common-mode direction rather than loss of relative alpha?
4. Does removing common cross-sectional mean exposure expose a stable residual ranking signal, or does information disappear entirely?
5. Is the LONG/SHORT asymmetry robust after conditioning on score magnitude/rank with predeclared quantiles?
6. Are failures concentrated in entries, ongoing rebalances, or persistent holdings once normalized by exposure duration?
7. Can any explanation be stated economically before selecting its threshold?

## Evidence/provenance

8. Which post-boundary rows now have fully matured 12h outcomes, and can they be proven uninspected?
9. Are all features used for a future candidate reconstructable strictly from timestamps <= decision time?
10. Is there enough independent time coverage for a three-fold held-out evaluation, or should trial 871 remain unconsumed?

## Falsification questions

11. What result would make us retire H12 rather than keep filtering it?
12. Does a proposed regime effect keep the same sign in >=2 chronological development folds?
13. Does the effect survive 10/20bps and a relevant delay without needing a parameter rescue?
14. Is the proposed mechanism genuinely distinct from H1-H8 and V10 flat-trend scaling?
