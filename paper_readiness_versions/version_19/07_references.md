# References — V19

1. Binance Public Data / USD-M `daily/metrics` archive: historical 5-minute futures metrics including open interest and trader-positioning ratios.
2. Binance USD-M market-data API documentation: `openInterestHist`, global/top-trader long-short ratio and taker buy/sell volume endpoints establish the corresponding market-data concepts.
3. Dunbar & Owusu-Amoako (2023), *Predictability of crypto returns: The impact of trading behavior*, Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Finance 39, 100812 — reports predictive relationships between futures participant trading behavior and cryptocurrency returns; useful as mechanism motivation, not as proof for this universe/horizon.
